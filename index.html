Mochitsuki

餅つきMochi pounding

蒸したもち米を、杵と臼でつきます。合いの手に合わせて交代しながら、つきあがったら手で丸めて、きなこやあんこで。Pound steamed rice in a wooden mortar, taking turns with the family. When it's smooth, shape it by hand and eat it with kinako or sweet bean paste.

畑と田んぼFields and paddies

春から秋にかけて、その時期だけの農作業を。日程は天候と育ち具合で前後します。Seasonal farm work from spring to autumn. Dates move with the weather and the crop.
